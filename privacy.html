NinjaParkour Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What we collect

NinjaParkour is a casual mini game. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal details.

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked characters, upgrades, achievements and settings) on your own device using the storage provided by the platform. This data is not sent to our servers.

Platform data

The game runs inside TikTok. When you play, TikTok may share basic information with the game, such as an anonymous player identifier, as described in TikTok's own privacy policy. We do not use this information to identify you.

Advertising and analytics

The game does not currently show third-party advertising and does not use third-party analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can reset your saved progress by clearing the game's data from within TikTok.
